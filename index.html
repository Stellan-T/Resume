About me

I'm a third-year Artificial Intelligence student at the University of Amsterdam (GPA 8.5) with a deep fascination for how machines learn, how intelligent systems are built, and where the boundaries of the field can still be pushed.

What keeps me hooked is the pace. Every few months there's a new model, a new technique, a new use case that genuinely amazes me — I'm not chasing one fixed destination in AI, I'm passionate about the whole field and its future, and I want to be building at that edge as it moves.

I thrive where analytical thinking meets creative problem-solving. Right now both get a daily workout as a Working Student AI at REVOCIVIC — and at the gym a different kind of workout altogether: show up, put in the reps, push a little further. Wired for logic and creativity, in whichever order the day demands.
